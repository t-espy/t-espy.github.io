Selected Projects

Strategy Assayer — AI-Assisted Strategy Credibility Platform

Private quantitative research platform built on QuantConnect LEAN to determine whether a strategy exhibits credible, out-of-sample edge or merely fits historical noise. The public lean-optimizer-public repository exposes its optimization engine: it compiles C# strategies once, runs warm Docker containers with persistent .NET harness processes, and searches parameter space using genetic, Latin Hypercube, Bayesian/Optuna TPE, and local-grid stages. The private platform adds IBKR data ingestion, WFO/WFAC credibility validation, portfolio construction, prediction bands, parameter-staleness monitoring, live/paper deployment packaging, dashboard approval, and post-trade analytics.

Strategy Assayer has evaluated seven strategy families across 257 symbols, run 5,563 research test plans and 36,254 walk-forward windows, and maintains more than 2,000 private automated tests. A bounded LLM-guided Research Steward can read results and queue follow-on experiments through restricted interfaces; live trading remains separate and manually approved. Current work includes evaluating AI/ML techniques for portfolio construction, adaptive position sizing, and research prioritization while preserving deterministic execution and human approval over capital allocation.

Open-Sourcing lean-optimizer: Design Decisions Behind a Parallel Optimization Engine

LinkedIn post announcing the open-source release of lean-optimizer and walking through its three core design decisions: running LEAN locally to eliminate cloud backtesting costs, compiling the C# strategy once and keeping warm Docker containers for ~1.8x throughput, and replacing exhaustive grid search with a genetic algorithm that converged in 611 evaluations against a 679,140-point parameter space.
