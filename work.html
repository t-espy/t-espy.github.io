Geospatial Data Engineering & Decision Support

Real Estate Market Intelligence

A private web application that a working real estate agent uses every week. It loads regular MLS exports, now more than 100,000 listings across north Georgia, into one local database and turns them into the agent's working material: expired-listing prospect letters with printed envelopes, comparative market analyses, area trend reports, farm-area sheets, and buyer briefs. The weekly prospect mailing goes from a fresh MLS export to addressed letters and envelopes in one step, with a sample sent straight to the office printer for review.

Private application; MLS and client data are not publicly exposed.
